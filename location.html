Getting Here

Easy to Reach, Easy to Return To

The lab sits directly on Thane-Belapur Road — Navi Mumbai’s main industrial corridor — with its own railway station close by.

A-74/9, TTC Industrial Area, Koparkhairane MIDC, Thane Belapur Road, Navi Mumbai (New Mumbai), India.

On Thane-Belapur Road

Direct road access from Thane, Airoli, Ghansoli, Vashi and beyond — no detours through inner lanes. All on the Thane-Belapur Road corridor, which runs from Thane down to the Sion-Panvel Highway.

Its Own Railway Station

Koparkhairane Station, on the Trans-Harbour Line, connects straight into the rest of Mumbai and Navi Mumbai.

A Proper Industrial Estate

Wide roads and ample parking inside TTC MIDC — an easy, unhurried weekend drop-off.
