Privacy Policy & Terms

How we handle the information you share with us, and the basic terms of booking a workshop, program or membership.

Privacy Policy

Future Makers ("we," "us") runs a hands-on STEM lab in Koparkhairane MIDC, Navi Mumbai. This page explains what information we collect when you contact us or enrol, and how we use it.

What we collect

When you call, WhatsApp, or otherwise reach out to Admissions, we may collect your name, phone number, your child’s name and age, and any other details you choose to share to process a workshop or program booking.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and confirm seats for a workshop, program batch, or membership
  • To send booking confirmations, schedule reminders, and updates about the batch you’ve joined
  • To keep basic enrolment and attendance records for the program

What we don’t do

We do not sell, rent, or share your contact details with third parties for marketing purposes. Information is used only to run and communicate about our own programs.

Cookies & tracking

This website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. The location page embeds a Google Maps iframe, which is subject to Google’s own privacy policy when loaded.

Data retention

We retain enquiry and enrolment details for as long as needed to deliver the program and maintain basic records, and will delete or anonymise them on request where we’re not required to keep them for legal or record-keeping reasons.

Your choices

To ask what information we hold about you or your child, or to request it be corrected or deleted, contact Admissions — Hardeep Kaur at 7977160233.

Terms of Enrolment

Fees & payment

The 2-Day 3D Printing Workshop is ₹3,000, credited toward registration if you enrol in the 6-Month STEM Innovation Program afterward. The 6-month program is ₹24,000 total (₹4,000/month, or ₹22,000 paid upfront). The Maker Access Membership is ₹2,000/month, optional and available after completing the program.

Seats & batches

Batches are capped at 10 children and confirmed on a first-come, first-served basis once payment is received. We recommend booking ahead, as batches do fill up.

Age & eligibility

The 6-month program and general lab access are open to children aged 6 and above. The 2-Day 3D Printing Workshop is designed for ages 10–16, school students.

Changes

These terms may be updated from time to time; the current version on this page applies. For any questions about a specific booking, please contact Admissions directly.
