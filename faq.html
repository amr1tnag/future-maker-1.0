FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything families ask before enrolling.

Do I need any prior experience?

No — no experience needed, beginners are welcome.

What age group is this for?

Ages 6 and above for the overall lab and 6-month program; the 3D printing workshop is for ages 10–16, school students.

What will I take home from the workshop?

Your keychain, your team project, all your design files, and a certificate.

Does the workshop fee count toward the full program?

Yes — it’s credited toward your registration if you join the 6-month program afterward.

How many seats are there per batch?

Seats are limited — each batch is capped at 10 children.

What happens after the 6-month program ends?

Children can join the Maker Access Membership — pay-per-use lab access at ₹2,000/month for 4 lab-hours — to keep building.

Where is Future Makers located?

Inside Koparkhairane MIDC, Navi Mumbai — on Thane Belapur Road, with its own railway station (Koparkhairane, Trans-Harbour Line) close by.

How do I enrol?

Contact Admissions (Hardeep Kaur — 7977160233) or WhatsApp (Mansi — +91 88509 63733).

Still have questions?

Reach out to admissions directly — we’re happy to help.
